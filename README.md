# Peaky Blinders

![Peaky Blinders](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/3/3f/Peaky_Blinders_season_6_poster.jpg/220px-Peaky_Blinders_season_6_poster.jpg)

**Peaky Blinders** is a British *crime drama* series set in **Birmingham, England**, in the years after World War I.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail   | Info                  |
|----------|-----------------------|
| Creator  | Steven Knight         |
| Network  | BBC                   |
| Aired    | 2013 – 2022           |
| Seasons  | 6                     |
| Genre    | Crime, Drama, Period  |

## Main Characters

- **Thomas "Tommy" Shelby** (Cillian Murphy)
- **Arthur Shelby** (Paul Anderson)
- **Polly Gray** (Helen McCrory)
- **Ada Shelby** (Sophie Rundle)
- **John Shelby** (Joe Cole)

## Why I Like It

1. The storytelling is gripping and full of twists.
2. The cinematography and costumes look amazing.
3. The soundtrack gives every scene a unique mood.
4. Tommy Shelby is a complex, unforgettable lead.

## Famous Catchphrase
